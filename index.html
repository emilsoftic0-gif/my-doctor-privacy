1. Introduction

AI Doctor Educator ("we", "our", or "the app") is an educational tool that provides general health and wellness information for learning purposes. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

Important: AI Doctor Educator is NOT a medical device or service. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical concerns.

2. Information We Access

To provide core functionality, our app may access the following device features:

  • Camera: Used only when you choose to take a photo to include in a conversation.
  • Microphone: Used only when you choose to use voice input to speak your question.
  • Files & Storage: Used only when you choose to attach a file or image to a conversation.
  • Internet: Required to communicate with our AI service provider to generate responses.

3. How We Process Information

Your conversations (text, images, voice input) are processed as follows:

  • Your messages are sent to our third-party AI service provider (OpenAI) solely to generate responses. OpenAI's privacy policy is available at openai.com/policies/privacy-policy.
  • We do not store your messages, images, or voice recordings on our own servers.
  • Conversation history is stored locally on your device only for your convenience.
  • We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Data Retention

  • Conversation history is stored locally on your device using an internal database.
  • You can delete your conversation history at any time within the app (via the "Delete" button in the history menu).
  • We do not retain any of your data on external servers.

5. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

  • OpenAI API: To generate AI responses. Messages you send are transmitted to OpenAI for processing.
  • Google Play Billing: For optional premium subscriptions. Payment information is processed entirely by Google Play and is not accessible to us.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with information, please contact us at emilsoftic0@gmail.com.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your conversation history (it is stored locally on your device).
  • Delete your conversation history at any time.
  • Uninstall the app, which will remove all locally stored data.
  • Contact us with any privacy-related questions or concerns.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
