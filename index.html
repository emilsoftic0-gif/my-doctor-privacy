Privacy Policy for My Doctor

Last updated: 12. mаy 2026

1. Information We Collect

Our app may collect the following types of information:

  • Camera: Used to take photos for sending in chat.
  • Microphone: Used for voice input when you speak your questions.
  • Files: You can send files (text, images) in chat.

All content you provide is sent to our AI service provider (OpenAI) solely to generate responses. We do not store your messages or files on our servers.

2. How We Use Information

The information is used solely to:

  • Process your questions and provide AI-generated answers.
  • Maintain your conversation history locally on your device for your convenience.
  • Improve the app experience.

3. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties, except as necessary to provide the service (e.g., sending messages to OpenAI). We do not sell your data.

4. Data Retention

Messages are stored locally on your device using an internal database. You can delete conversation history at any time within the app. No messages are stored on our servers.

5. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
